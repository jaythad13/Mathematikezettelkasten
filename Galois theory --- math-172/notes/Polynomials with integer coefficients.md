We already know $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ [[Abstract algebra --- math-171/notes/Unique factorisation#_examples _ Euclidean domains|is a Euclidean domain]], and thus, a PID. We will see that $\mathbb{Z}[x]$ is in fact a [[Abstract algebra --- math-171/notes/Unique factorisation#_definition _ unique factorisation domains|UFD]]. In fact, we will see that $\mathbb{Z}[x]$ and $\mathbb{Q}[x]$ are very closely related. This is a special case of a more general fact about [[Abstract algebra --- math-171/notes/Unique factorisation#_proposition _ UFDs are preserved by localisation|localising UFDs]].
