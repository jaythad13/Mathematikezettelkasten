Suppose we have two categories $\mathscr{S}, \mathscr{T}$ and a pair of adjoint functors $\Sigma, \Omega$ between them. The case we are most concerned with is when $\mathscr{S} = \mathscr{T} = \mathsf{Top}_{*}$ (based topological spaces). Note, we have natural transformations $\eta : \operatorname{id} \to \Omega \Sigma$ and $\varepsilon : \Sigma \Omega \to \operatorname{id}$. As they do for any pair of adjoint functors, we see that $\Omega \Sigma$ gives a [[UChicago --- uc-2026/notes/Monads#_definition _ monad|monad]] on $\mathscr{T}$.
